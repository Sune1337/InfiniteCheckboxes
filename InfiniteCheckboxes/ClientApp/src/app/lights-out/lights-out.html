Click a box to flip it on or off. When you flip a box, all adjacent boxes will be flipped as well. The goal is to uncheck all boxes.

Since you can simply learn how to solve this puzzle, there's no highscore list. That also means if you want a brain-teaser, don't use FavouriteSearchEngine™!
